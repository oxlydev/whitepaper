# 1 Abstract

Oxly is a non-custodial decentralized exchange protocol built on an on-chain order book and deployed across the BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum, and Solana ecosystems—the networks with the highest concentration of on-chain trading activity. Users trade directly from self-custodial wallets using limit and market orders. Each order is locked in an escrow smart contract until it is matched peer-to-peer on-chain. The entire transaction lifecycle—order creation, execution, cancellation, and burn—is transparent and publicly auditable on block explorers. Because no third party ever holds user funds, Oxly eliminates the single point of failure that exposes centralized venues to hacks and asset freezes.

Oxly brings together on a single platform the capabilities that traders and developers currently have to source from separate venues. This matters for growth: every developer who lists a token or appears on Oxly Live brings their own community to the platform, and every new community adds traders, volume, and demand for OXLY.

* **Spot Order Book DEX Across Three Networks** — Delivers execution on par with centralized exchanges—limit orders, maker/taker pricing, instant cancellation, and professional charting—with full self-custody and without bridges or new networks.
* **Perpetual Futures DEX** — Offers USDT-margined perpetual contracts with up to 1000x leverage, cross and isolated margin, and Take Profit / Stop Loss (TP/SL), backed by the Oxly futures liquidity pool.
* **Trading Without a Gas Token** — On BNB Smart Chain, staked OXLY generates Oxly Gas (OXG), which covers the full gas cost of every order placement and cancellation.
* **Self-Listing on the Order Book** — Any project can list its token permissionlessly on all three networks for a flat fee with automated review, and immediately receive a live order book market.
* **Mobile App with a Self-Custody Wallet** — Native iOS and Android apps with an integrated self-custody wallet and QR-code authentication for access to the web platform.
* **Oxly Live** — An integrated livestreaming platform that connects project teams directly with the trading community, with a participation-based incentive scheme that distributes OXLY rewards to the most engaged viewers.

**OXLY** is the ecosystem's core utility token, designed to be community-owned from a fair launch. Maximum supply is permanently capped at 1,000,000,000 OXLY, with no presale, no venture capital allocation, and no pre-mint. At launch, almost all of the initial circulating supply is distributed to the community through a participation-based airdrop program covering content contribution, social engagement, referrals, and trading activity. OXLY utility is then embedded across every product: staking for trading fee discounts and gas-free trading, listing fee payments, and Oxly Live incentives. 80% of all spot trading fee revenue across the three networks is allocated to buying back OXLY on the open market and burning it permanently, creating a deflationary mechanism that reduces total supply as platform adoption grows.
