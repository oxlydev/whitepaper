# 17 Risk Factors & Legal Disclaimer

## 17.1 Risk Factors

Participating in the Oxly ecosystem and holding or using OXLY involves technical, operational, market, and regulatory risks. Users must carefully understand and evaluate all of the following risk factors:

* **Smart Contract Risk:** Despite testing and code review, smart contracts may still contain hidden vulnerabilities or bugs that could cause execution failures or the permanent loss of funds.
* **Operator and Infrastructure Risk:** Technical disruptions to off-chain services or operator infrastructure may delay order matching, although user assets remain safely held in the escrow contract and orders can always be cancelled on-chain.
* **Multi-Chain Ecosystem Risk:** Each supported blockchain (BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum, and Solana) has different technical characteristics and is exposed to network congestion, outages, chain forks, and differences in transaction finality.
* **Third-Party Dependency Risk:** External supporting services—including wallet providers, RPC nodes, market data feed providers, and third-party platforms—may change specifications, restrict access, or fail operationally beyond Oxly's control.
* **Social Platform Policy Risk:** Changes to X's terms of service, API access restrictions, pricing changes, or enforcement against rewarded engagement campaigns may limit or halt the Social Campaign program.
* **Market Volatility Risk:** Digital asset valuations are highly volatile and unpredictable. Leveraged futures trading carries high risk and can result in losses exceeding the initial margin, and OXLY may lose part or all of its market value.
* **Liquidity Risk:** Order book depth on certain trading pairs may be thin, especially for newly listed tokens, potentially causing significant slippage.
* **Protocol Parameter Change Risk:** Certain operational parameters—including fee rates, discount tier structures, OXG budget allocation, service rates, and reward distribution schemes—may be adjusted by the protocol owner within the boundaries permitted by the smart contracts.
* **Regulatory and Compliance Risk:** Legal and regulatory frameworks for digital assets and decentralized derivatives trading vary across jurisdictions and may change at any time, potentially affecting the accessibility or legality of using the platform in certain regions.
* **Key Management Risk:** As a self-custody system, users are fully responsible for securing their own wallet private keys and seed phrases; lost or compromised keys cannot be recovered by anyone.
